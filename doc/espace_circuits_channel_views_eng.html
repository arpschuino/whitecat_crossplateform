Channel Views

Channel Views let you customize the display of the channel space. They can be enabled and disabled on the fly.

In Channel Views mode, only circuits present in at least one active view are accessible — with the mouse or the keyboard.

Editing a Channel View from a circuit selection

The first Channel View (« Patched ») is read-only — it automatically reflects the content of the patch. The 15 other Channel Views are freely configurable by the user.

Create a Channel View

  • Select your circuits in the [Classical] channel space
  • Press [STORE] ( [F1] )
  • click the Channel View slot

Remove circuits from a Channel View

  • Select the circuits
  • click [Modify] or press [F2]
  • click the Channel View slot

Add circuits to a Channel View

This operation can be done from [Classical] mode or from other Views.

  • Select the circuits
  • click [Modify] or press [F2]
  • click the destination Channel View slot

Move circuits from one Channel View to another

  • Select the circuits
  • click [Report] or press [F3]
  • click the destination Channel View slot

Give a label to a Channel View

  • Enable text mode with the [F5] key
  • Type the label
  • click the text slot of the Channel View

Generate a Channel View from faders or memories

This Channel View generation is static.

If you create a Channel View from a memory, and later add or remove circuits from that memory, the Channel View will not be updated — it is not linked dynamically.

Generate from a memory

  • Enable the [M] button of the Channel View
  • Type the memory number (must exist in the cue list)
  • click [Store] or press [F1]
  • click [Build View]

Generate from a fader

  • Enable the [F] button of the Channel View
  • Type the fader number
  • click [Store] or press [F1]
  • click [Build View]

The view will be built from the active dock. A dynamic state (chaser, trichromie, gridplayers) will be captured at the moment of saving: circuits above 0 will be included.

Generate from all memories

  • Enable the [M] button of the Channel View
  • Enable the [ALL] button
  • click [Store] or press [F1]
  • click [Build View]

Generate from all faders

  • Enable the [F] button of the Channel View
  • Enable the [ALL] button
  • click [Store] or press [F1]
  • click [Build View]

Generate from all faders and memories

  • Enable both [M] and [F] buttons of the Channel View
  • Enable the [ALL] button
  • click [Store] or press [F1]
  • click [Build View]

Clear a Channel View

  • click [Clear] or press [F4]
  • click the Channel View slot

MIDI assignments

The [Classical] button and the 16 views can be assigned to MIDI controls.

The same applies to the channel space scrollbar handle.

See MIDI Configuration and Assignments
