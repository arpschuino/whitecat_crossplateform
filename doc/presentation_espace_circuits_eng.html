Presentation of the channel space

There are 3 display modes for the channels:

  • The Classical view
  • The Patched view (patched channels)
  • The customizable views in Channel Views

You can switch from one to another using the [Classical] and [Patched] buttons, or by activating the small squares representing the 15 customizable views.

Classical mode

The classical view shows every channel that can be controlled in WhiteCat. You can select or deselect all the channels. You can navigate through this view using the scrolling handle.

Patched mode

The patched view shows all the patched channels. Unpatched channels do not appear and cannot be selected.

Channel Views mode

Channel Views let you build customized views and tidy up your channel workspace the way you like.

The 15 views can be edited, based on:

  • a selection of channels made in the [Classical] space
  • a memory
  • a fader
  • all the memories
  • all the faders
  • all the memories and faders together

When you are in a Channel View, you cannot select a channel that is not in the active views.
